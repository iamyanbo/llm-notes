The field of LLMs is moving quickly, and there are many more ideas I would like to write about. These are the essays I have written for now. I hope to return to the questions we have left open and to other architectures that deserve the same careful attention.

If an idea here has caught your interest, I hope you will follow it beyond these pages: read the original paper, work through its calculations, or try an implementation of your own. You may find a better way to explain it, a limitation worth investigating, or a construction we have not considered. Perhaps these notes will help you begin something special of your own. I would be glad of that.
